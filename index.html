Explore New Waters

Explore New Waters

From Gulf Coast kayak launches to bridge structures and barrier islands — every spot tells its own story.

Pensacola, FL

Excellent pier access, deep channels, and offshore trolling reefs. Famous for Spanish mackerel and red snapper in the bay.

Best Season: Summer / Fall
Top pick: Matrix Shad ‘Lemon Head’ for Spanish mackerel in clear Gulf water — Shop Now

Biloxi, MS

Shallow bayous, tidal reefs, and bridge structures. Prime spot for Speckled Trout, Black Drum, and Flounder in deep structure.

Best Season: Spring / Fall
Top pick: Matrix Shad ‘Avocado’ for speckled trout in murky bayous — Shop Now

Ship Island, MS

Barrier island with deep gulches and crystal clear surf. Ideal for shorecasting and offshore kayak angling for bull redfish.

Best Season: Early Fall
Top pick: Matrix Shad ‘Tiger Bait’ for bull redfish in stained surf — Shop Now

Pontchartrain, LA

Giant brackish lake crossed by concrete bridges. The massive bridge pylons attract trophy-sized trout and sheepshead.

Best Season: Winter / Spring
Top pick: Matrix Shad ‘Green Hornet’ for trophy trout around bridge pylons — Shop Now

ONE TRIP ONE BITE

Every journey has a destination, but the destination is never the whole story.

One Trip One Bite was created from a simple belief: every trip should leave you with something worth remembering. Sometimes it's the fish you've been chasing all day. Sometimes it's a hidden restaurant you never expected to find. Sometimes it's a historic landmark, a breathtaking view, a local tradition, or a special moment shared with family and friends.

We travel to discover. We fish to explore. We wander to understand the places, people, food, history, and nature that make every destination unique.

From coastal roads and fishing piers to beaches, small towns, local restaurants, museums, parks, and historic sites, every adventure becomes an opportunity to learn something new and create a story worth telling.

Whether the reward is a trophy fish, a memorable meal, a hidden gem, or a meaningful experience with the people we love, every journey brings back a discovery.

Because at the end of the day, it's not about how far you travel. It's about what you find along the way.

One Trip. One Bite. One Story.
