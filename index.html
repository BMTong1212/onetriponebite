Explore New Waters

Coastal Fishing Spot Guides

A collection of kayak launches, fishing bridge locations, and seasonal insights for anglers seeking adventure.

Pensacola, FL

Excellent pier access, deep channels, and offshore trolling reefs. Famous for Spanish mackerel and red snapper in the bay.

Best Season: Summer / Fall

Biloxi, MS

Shallow bayous, tidal reefs, and bridge structures. Prime spot for Speckled Trout, Black Drum, and Flounder in deep structure.

Best Season: Spring / Fall

Ship Island, MS

Barrier island with deep gulches and crystal clear surf. Ideal for shorecasting and offshore kayak angling for bull redfish.

Best Season: Early Fall

Pontchartrain, LA

Giant brackish lake crossed by concrete bridges. The massive bridge pylons attract trophy-sized trout and sheepshead.

Best Season: Winter / Spring
