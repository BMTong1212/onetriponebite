🔒 Security

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026  |  Effective from: May 28, 2026

✅ Our Commitment: One Trip One Bite respects your privacy. We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase products on One Trip One Bite, we collect the following details:

1.1. Information you provide directly

  • Full Name: To personalize delivery emails and invoices.
  • Delivery Email: To send the product files and order confirmations.
  • Phone Number (optional): To contact you for customer support if needed.
  • Payment Details: PayPal transaction ID (we do NOT store card numbers or full financial details).

1.2. Information collected automatically

  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system — used solely to analyze technical performance and logs.
  • Local Storage: We use localStorage to save the countdown timer state (contains no personal info).

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used for the following purposes:

  • ✉️ Sending product files via email after successful payment.
  • 📋 Logging orders in our management system (internal Google Sheets database).
  • 🔔 Sending free product updates (if any).
  • 🛠 Providing customer and technical support.
  • 📊 Analyzing overall sales statistics (anonymized data).

⚠️ We never use your email to send spam, third-party ads, or any unauthorized marketing communications.

3. Information Security

We apply robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your data:

  • 🔐 Secure HTTPS connection encrypts all data transmission.
  • 🗄 Order data is stored securely on Google Sheets with restricted access.
  • 💳 PayPal payments are handled entirely by PayPal — we never see or store your credit/debit card numbers.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

We only share information in the following limited cases:

  • Payment Processors: PayPal (to safely process transactions).
  • Google Services: Order details are stored on Google Sheets and emails are sent via Gmail API (Google Apps Script) — subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal Requirements: When requested by competent law enforcement agencies under applicable laws.

Outside of these cases, we **never share** your personal information with anyone else.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • 📋 Right of Access: Request to view the information we store about you.
  • ✏️ Right of Rectification: Request updates to inaccurate information.
  • 🗑 Right of Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal data after the transaction has been completed.
  • 📧 Right to Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from future product updates at any time by replying to our email with "Unsubscribe".

To exercise any of these rights, please contact: support@onetriponebite.com

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses localStorage (browser-based storage, not tracking cookies) to:

  • Store the countdown timer state.
  • Remember remaining available slots (for visual styling and countdowns).

This data is stored purely locally on your device, is never transmitted to our servers, and can be cleared at any time by deleting your browser history.

7. Data Retention Policy

We retain order details for **24 months** from the date of purchase to handle customer support issues and deliver free template updates. After this period, data is deleted or anonymized.

8. Contact & Support

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

📧 Email: support@onetriponebite.com
⏰ Response Time: Within 24–48 business hours (Monday – Friday)

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any major changes will be emailed to existing customers. The last updated date will always be visible at the top of this page.

📅 This policy is effective as of May 28, 2026. Version 1.0.
